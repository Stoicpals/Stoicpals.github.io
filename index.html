Real-Time Color Matrix Encoder

Key Features

  • Image-to-text and text-to-image transformation.
  • Multiple encoding modes, including ASCII, Numeric, Hex and Emoji.
  • Color-aware visual representation and reconstruction.
  • Live webcam and video processing.
  • Interactive encoding controls and visual previews.

My Contribution: Contributed to UI design and frontend development as part of an academic group project. Also developed an understanding of encoding and decoding concepts, including the transformation of visual information into symbolic representations and its reconstruction into images.

Technologies: Next.js, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, WebGL and Web Workers.
